# Rails Girls São Paulo

Welcome to the **[Rails Girls São Paulo](https://railsgirls.com.br/)** guide! 💜

Rails Girls is a free, one-day workshop for women, trans people and non-binary people who want to take their first steps in programming. At an in-person workshop, held in the city of São Paulo, we build a real app together, using [Ruby on Rails](https://guia.railsgirls.com.br/glossario.html#rails), and this guide goes along with every step, during the workshop and after it.

Rails Girls São Paulo wants to be a welcoming, safe and inclusive space for everyone. That is why everyone who takes part follows our [code of conduct](https://railsgirls.com.br/codigo-de-conduta/) (in Portuguese).

## Who this guide is for

**For participants.** You don't need to know anything about programming. The guide explains every step and every term, and you can follow it at your own pace: on the workshop day, with the mentoring team nearby, or on your own, at home, whenever you like.

**For mentors.** The [Mentoring guide](https://guia.railsgirls.com.br/mentoras/) has the mentoring approach, the schedule for the day, the most common errors and notes for each chapter of the project.

**For anyone who wants to learn.** The workshop is for women, trans people and non-binary people, but this guide is open: anyone can follow the chapters on their own, for free. And if it helps you, share it with more people. 💜

## Where to start

1. Create your GitHub account, if you don't have one yet: [Creating a GitHub account](https://guia.railsgirls.com.br/comece-aqui/conta-no-github.html). You can do it on the workshop day itself.
2. If you like, read the basics of [Ruby, Rails and Git](https://guia.railsgirls.com.br/comece-aqui/ruby-rails-e-git.html). It takes about 10 minutes.
3. Grab a pen and paper and start the project with the [Message Board](https://guia.railsgirls.com.br/projetos/mural-de-recados/). 🚀

You don't need to read the rest of the guide first. It's there for you to look things up: the project explains each thing at the moment it comes up.
